# O2C Process Change Log

This document represents internal operational context that an analyst could consult after identifying a data pattern. It is intentionally separate from the hidden Scenario V1 ground truth.

## 2024-07-01 — Marketplace promotional review step

A manual promotional-code review step was introduced for Marketplace orders beginning July 1, 2024.

The review was added to confirm promotion eligibility before an order is released to fulfillment. The initial rollout did not change the formal customer shipping SLA.

Operations expected most reviews to be completed within one business day. No formal post-implementation cycle-time assessment had been completed at launch.

This change log records timing and process design only. It does not establish that the new review step caused any later performance change.

## 2024-08-15 — Collections reminder template update

Finance Operations updated the wording of standard payment reminder emails for customers with open invoices.

The update did not change contractual payment terms, credit limits, invoice due dates, or the escalation schedule.

No specific cycle-time impact was assumed when the template was changed.

## 2024-09-01 — Warehouse staffing schedule normalization

The Phoenix and Dallas distribution centers returned to their standard staffing schedules after summer vacation coverage.

No warehouse-capacity expansion or major carrier-contract change was recorded for this date.

## Interpretation guidance

Process changes are contextual evidence, not proof of causality. Analysts should combine change-log timing with measured process data, affected cohorts, alternative explanations, and—where possible—controlled or quasi-experimental evidence before making a causal claim.
