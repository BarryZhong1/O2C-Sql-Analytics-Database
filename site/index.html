Sample recommendation

FINDING Q3 O2C cycle time worsened versus Q2. Order → Ship deteriorated most, and the increase is concentrated in Marketplace. BUSINESS CONTEXT A manual Marketplace promotional-code review step began July 1, 2024. The timing supports a hypothesis, but it does not prove the change caused the slowdown. WHAT-IF SCENARIO A 25% Order → Ship improvement reduces the modeled cash cycle from 33.58 to 33.06 days, saving about 0.52 days. NEXT ACTION Pilot a targeted Marketplace order-release improvement. Track Order → Ship time, exception/rework rate, SLA attainment, and total O2C cycle time. Review pilot evidence before broad rollout.
